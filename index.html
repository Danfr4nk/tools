tools

Every instrument, one page. Older versions live as sub-links under the current one.

workbench

One photo in, every instrument out — shared face detection fans out to age estimation and facial telemetry in a single pass, with one unified report. Nothing is uploaded.

kinship

Sibling check — compares the most prominent face in each of two photos and estimates whether the two people could be genetically related: similarity score, confidence, plain-language verdict. Runs in the browser, nothing is uploaded.

age

Visual age estimation — Vision Transformer age-bracket classifier, runs 100% in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

body

Telemetry mannequin — import a breast_telemetry/v1 JSON and the bust rebuilds to scale on a neutral grey mannequin, in 3D and 2D. Nothing is uploaded.

modbod

Anonymized parametric wireframe body study — sculpt bust, waist, hips, glute, thigh, height on a statistical mesh with per-vertex source-material coverage. Single page, no uploads.

co-watch

Watch something with Sammy — send a YouTube link in chat, he reads the timed transcript and talks it through with you in real time.

attraction

preference diagnostic

Iterative A/B mapping of what you find attractive in a face — Phase 1 sweeps structural archetypes, Phase 2 isolates one variable at a time, every pick scored on measured metric deltas against the 155-face bank.

telemetry lab

Standalone precision facial measurement — ~40-metric vector: gonial angles, facial thirds, fWHR, canthal tilt, regional asymmetry, head pose. Telestrator overlays, 95% intervals, JSON/CSV export.

body metrics

Precision full-body measurement in-browser — 16 scale-invariant body ratios from pose landmarks, skeleton overlays, per-person models, z-scored matching with per-ratio breakdowns.

frame describe

Open-ended body/frame analysis in your own lexicon — upload a photo, get an exhaustive qualitative read. Bring your own vision API key.

scenario ratings

234 rated situations: 78 bases × 2 single-knob modifiers. Each modifier changes exactly one thing, so the readout is causal. ∅ vetoes a question out of scoring; ✎ adds a private note per line.

scenario telemetry

Named-preset scenario bouts, then metric isolation — run the bout, then every trial shows only what's different between A and B. Veto both, reluctant, or pick a side.

face book

The browsable catalog of the whole face library — every synthetic face in the bank, filterable by phase, axis, and group, with lightbox inspection.

music

MusicTrainer

Weekly scoring instrument for the taste experiment — Discover Weekly + Release Radar scorecards. Per track: 1–10 score, skip/like, ADDED? yes/no. Prediction paste with timestamped lock, week JSON export/import.

AUTOPSY

Driver-dissection game — answers what it is about the track that did it. Per track: triage + score, WHAT DID IT? check-all-that-apply across 16 drivers, KILL ONE load-bearing-element pick. Drivers ranked by lift over base keep rate.

hook2piano

TheoryTab → one-page piano sheet. Paste a TheoryTab URL or tab ID, get a printable grand-staff score — left hand plays the chords, right hand the melody, with chord symbols and Roman numerals. Runs in the browser via Pyodide.

MELODY

Upload a finished song — it finds the lead melody, puts it on a piano roll, plays it back, and exports MIDI for your DAW. Runs 100% on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
