Driver-dissection game — answers what it is about the track that did it. Per track: triage + score, WHAT DID IT? check-all-that-apply across 16 drivers, KILL ONE load-bearing-element pick. Drivers ranked by lift over base keep rate.

TheoryTab → one-page piano sheet. Paste a TheoryTab URL or tab ID, get a printable grand-staff score — left hand plays the chords, right hand the melody, with chord symbols and Roman numerals. Runs in the browser via Pyodide.

MELODY

Upload a finished song — it finds the lead melody, puts it on a piano roll, plays it back, and exports MIDI for your DAW. Runs 100% on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
