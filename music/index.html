music

Taste diagnostics, training instruments, and music tools.

MusicTrainer

Weekly scoring instrument for the taste experiment — Discover Weekly + Release Radar scorecards. Per track: 1–10 score, skip/like, ADDED? yes/no. Prediction paste with timestamped lock, week JSON export/import.

AUTOPSY

Driver-dissection game — answers what it is about the track that did it. Per track: triage + score, WHAT DID IT? check-all-that-apply across 16 drivers, KILL ONE load-bearing-element pick. Drivers ranked by lift over base keep rate.

hook2piano

TheoryTab → one-page piano sheet. Paste a TheoryTab URL or tab ID, get a printable grand-staff score — left hand plays the chords, right hand the melody, with chord symbols and Roman numerals. Runs in the browser via Pyodide.

MELODY

Upload a finished song — it finds the lead melody, puts it on a piano roll, plays it back, and exports MIDI for your DAW. Runs 100% on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

PROGRESSIONS

Chord progression generator in the harmonic lanes of nimino, oskar med k, and LYNY — pick a lane, generate a voice-led 4-chord loop, audition it in the browser, export MIDI for your DAW.

CHORDS

Upload a finished song — it finds the tempo, tracks the beats, and names the chord on each one, drawn as a tap-to-hear timeline. The harmonic sibling of MELODY. Runs 100% on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
