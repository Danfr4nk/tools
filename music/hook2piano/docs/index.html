hook2piano

TheoryTab → printable piano sheet (LH chords, RH melody)
Paste a full theorytab/view/… link to pick from its sections, or a bare tab ID (e.g. nZgWr__wmry). Pasting a link goes through a public relay that rate-limits, so it can fail — the reliable path is the bookmarklet: open any TheoryTab page and tap ⚡ hook2piano (drag it to your bookmarks bar on desktop). It reads the tab list straight from the page, no relay needed. First load downloads the Python engine (~10 MB), then everything runs on your phone.
