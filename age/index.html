Method: Vision Transformer (ViT-Base) fine-tuned on FairFace age brackets, run locally via Transformers.js. The headline number is the probability-weighted mean of bracket midpoints — treat it as ± one bracket. FairFace-trained models carry dataset bias; results skew least-bad on clear, front-facing, well-lit faces. This estimates apparent age, not identity — it cannot tell you who someone is.
