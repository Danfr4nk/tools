What this measures. Every scenario below is generated from a 24-metric vector — the telemetry-lab treatment: each stimulus has known measurements, and your responses reveal which metrics actually drive your willingness. The instrument assumes you may hold several disconnected themes, not one coherent preference — so instead of forcing a single shape, it clusters your accepted scenarios and reports each theme separately, with its distance from your Oct 2019 baseline. Instructions: per pair, tap the scenario you'd be more willing to do, or veto both. Tap reluctant under a pick if you'd do it but barely. The purple line under each pair names the metrics being tested that trial.
