face timeline

Facial telemetry over time. Upload photos from different eras — each face gets the full telemetry scan, and the timeline computes how the measured face changed: step-by-step and against the first photo. Everything runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

save / load

The timeline auto-saves to this browser (thumbnails, metrics, dating — annotated overlays are memory-only and re-render on a fresh scan).

Detection: SCRFD (buffalo_l) — largest face per photo is measured. Telemetry: the 17-ratio MediaPipe FaceLandmarker diagnostic set from the attraction lab (scale-invariant, so different photo distances compare). Aggregate deviance is a descriptive Euclidean distance over per-metric relative deltas — not a model of aging. First run downloads models; everything is cached by your browser afterwards.
