What you are looking at: an approximation, not a body scan and not optical mocap. The motion is MediaPipe Pose (complexity 1, 281 pose runs over the swing window, zero detection failures, mean landmark visibility 0.94–0.96) from one 30 fps phone camera, interpolated and lightly smoothed. Depth is estimated by the network, not measured. The figure is a stock MakeHuman athletic base mesh with procedural clothes, not Dan's body. The club's angle in the image plane comes from the video. Its depth component comes from a swing-plane model, and the hand and face orientation follow from it. Hip sway comes from a camera solve against the video. The source camera sits behind the golfer, so it is a down-the-line view. Good enough to study the swing, not a frame-perfect measurement.
